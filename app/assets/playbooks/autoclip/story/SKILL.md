# Story / case study

A recipe, not rules: the user's request wins.

- **Hook:** start in the middle of the tension ("In 2019, the shop had 3 days of cash left.") — not with background.
- **Arc:** situation → problem (make it concrete and personal) → attempts/tension → the turn (the decision or
  discovery) → result with a number → lesson in one line.
- **Visuals:** one recurring visual anchor (a character, an object, a map, a graph line) that changes as the story
  moves, so the viewer feels progress. Dates, places and numbers appear as big type when spoken.
- **Voice:** past tense, short sentences, specific details; no corporate language.
- **Brand case studies:** the customer is the hero, the product is the tool they used; show the real product where
  the story reaches it.
- **End:** the lesson, then the call to action.
