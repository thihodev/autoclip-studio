How it works

From a sentence
to a posted video.

Four steps. You decide what the video is about and whether it's good — Claude does the rest.

01 — DESCRIBE

Say what the video is about

A topic, an article, a product page or a GitHub repo. Pick a format and length — or let Claude choose the structure and the look.

  • Two engines: Claude Motion (animated, 10–20 min) or Quick (~1 min)
  • 11 video types and 43 visual styles
  • Brand kits: logo, colors, fonts, music, tone
localhost:8000
02 — CLAUDE MAKES IT

Watch it get made, scene by scene

Claude writes the storyboard, records the voice-over, sends a designer to animate each scene in code, then checks every frame for cut-off text, contrast and safe zones before rendering.

  • Live steps, previews and Claude's own notes
  • Fits the TikTok feed: hook in the first second, nothing under the app's buttons

03 — REVIEW

Point at the frame, say what to change

Scrub the filmstrip, click a spot or drag a range and leave a note. Claude applies the whole round in one new version and only touches what you pointed at.

  • Comments pinned to a moment, a range or a spot
  • Every version kept — compare and roll back
AI & review
04 — PUBLISH

Post it — or schedule it

Claude writes the caption and hashtags and makes three covers. Connect TikTok in one click and post with TikTok's own choices — audience, comments, Duet, Stitch, disclosure.

  • TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram & Facebook Reels
  • Autopilot with an approval queue and posting times

Publishing

One click to TikTok.
Nothing kept in between.

AutoClip Connect signs you in to TikTok so you don't need a developer app. It hands the access back, encrypted for your AutoClip only, and forgets it.

AutoClip on your computer

Makes the video, keeps your sign-in, uploads straight to TikTok.

AutoClip Connect

Completes the sign-in and renews access. Stores no tokens, videos or personal data.

TikTok

You approve AutoClip, then choose who sees each post, comments, Duet, Stitch and disclosure.

Encrypted for one appEach sign-in uses a one-time key made on your computer; only it can open the result.
Videos never pass through usUploads go from your computer to TikTok with your own access.
Labeled and in your controlEvery post is marked AI-generated and goes out only when you say so.

Get started

Up and running
in two minutes.

No Docker, no .env. The first start installs what it needs; everything else is connected from the web UI.

macOS, Linux or Windows with Python 3.11+ (Node 22 for Claude Motion — one click from the app)
Claude — sign in with your plan, use this computer's Claude Code login, or an API key
Optional — ElevenLabs for natural voices, Pixabay for stock clips, TikTok in one click
# get AutoClip Studio
$ git clone https://github.com/thihodev/autoclip-studio
$ cd autoclip-studio
# start it — opens http://localhost:8000
$ ./start.sh
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

AutoClip Studio is free and open source (MIT). Making videos uses your own Claude account — your subscription's usage or your API key — and any optional services you connect, like ElevenLabs.

Which Claude do I need?

Any of: signing in with a Claude plan from the app, the Claude Code login already on your computer, or an Anthropic API key. You choose; AutoClip never picks an account for you. Claude Motion works best with Opus.

Does my data leave my computer?

Your videos, projects and keys stay in AutoClip's data folder on your computer. It talks to Claude, and to the services you connect, directly. AutoClip Connect only completes TikTok sign-ins and renews access; it stores nothing.

Can it post to TikTok automatically?

Yes — connect TikTok in one click. Direct posts show TikTok's own choices (audience, comments, Duet, Stitch, commercial disclosure) every time; Autopilot videos wait for your approval and can go to your TikTok inbox as drafts so you can add a trending sound before posting.

What languages does it speak?

Scripts, voices and captions in English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Indonesian and more — with fonts that keep every diacritic intact. The app itself is in English and Vietnamese.

How long does a video take?

Quick videos take about a minute. Claude Motion designs and animates every scene and checks each frame, which usually takes 10–20 minutes — you can leave the page while it works.
