How it works

From a sentence
to a finished video.

You decide what the video is about, approve the idea and judge the result. Claude does the rest, and the next episode starts from what you liked.

01 — DESCRIBE

Say what the video is about

A topic, an article, a product page or a GitHub repo, and optionally a video whose format you like. Pick a format and length, or let Claude choose the structure and the look.

  • Two engines: Claude Motion (animated, 10–20 min) or Quick (~1 min)
  • 11 video types, 43 visual styles, or the format of any video you link
  • Brand kits: logo, colors, fonts, music, tone
localhost:8000
02 — APPROVE THE IDEA

Nothing is made until you say yes

Claude first proposes the idea: a title, what it's about, the opening line and the video scene by scene. Change any of it or ask for another idea; only the one you approve gets made. That's cheaper than finding out after a 15-minute render.

  • Edit the title, hook and every scene before anything runs
  • “Another idea” for a different angle on the same topic
  • In a project, Claude suggests what the series covers next
The idea
03 — CLAUDE MAKES IT

Watch it get made, scene by scene

Claude writes the storyboard, records the voice-over, sends a designer to animate each scene in code, then checks every frame for cut-off text, contrast and safe zones before rendering.

  • Live steps, previews and Claude's own notes
  • Fits the TikTok feed: hook in the first second, nothing under the app's buttons

04 — REVIEW

Point at the frame, say what to change

Scrub the filmstrip, click a spot or drag a range and leave a note. Claude applies the whole round in one new version and only touches what you pointed at.

  • Comments pinned to a moment, a range or a spot
  • Every version kept — compare and roll back
AI & review
05 — EDIT

Change it yourself, no Claude run

The animated video plays live in the Design tab: click any text to retype it, swap an image or a logo, fix a caption, change a font or a color everywhere. Too long a word, a missing accent or a blurry image is flagged before you render.

  • Texts, images, captions, fonts, colors and pauses, with undo
  • Checked for layout and readability before each render
  • Plus a CapCut-style timeline: trims, speed, looks, titles, music
Design
06 — EXPORT

Download it — or keep editing anywhere

Download the finished MP4, or export it as layers and carry on in CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut. Claude also writes the caption and hashtags and makes three covers.

  • Scenes as clips, captions on a transparent layer, audio stems, SRT, timeline
  • Caption, hashtags and cover thumbnails, ready to copy

Learn from a video

Seen one that works?
Make yours in its format.

Paste a TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook link, or upload the file. Claude studies how it's made; your video follows the same structure, rhythm and look with your own content.

MEASURED, THEN STUDIED

What makes it work, written down

AutoClip measures every hard cut and the main colors and takes a frame per shot. Claude reads the frames once and writes the format down: each beat with its timing, the hook, the pacing, the colors, type, layout, motion and captions, and a recipe to follow.

  • Pick it under “Like this video” when you make one
  • Save the result as a template and make the series from it
  • Its footage, characters, logos, words and music are never copied

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

AutoClip Studio is free and open source (MIT). Making videos uses your own Claude account — your subscription's usage or your API key — and any optional services you connect, like ElevenLabs.

Which Claude do I need?

Any of: signing in with a Claude plan from the app, the Claude Code login already on your computer, or an Anthropic API key. You choose; AutoClip never picks an account for you. Claude Motion works best with Opus.

Does my data leave my computer?

Your videos, projects and keys stay in AutoClip's data folder on your computer. It talks to Claude, and to the services you connect, directly — nothing goes through us.

Can it make a video like a TikTok I like?

Yes. Paste its link (or upload the file) under Templates → Learn from a video. Claude studies its format — structure, hook, pacing, colors, type, motion and sound, including whether anyone speaks — and your video follows it with your own content: a music-only TikTok gets a video with no voice-over. It never copies the creator's footage, characters, logos, words or music. Some sites block downloads; then save the video and upload it.

Do I see the idea before it's made?

Yes, with “Plan the idea first” on (the default): Claude proposes the title, the opening line and the video scene by scene, you edit it or ask for another, and only the idea you approve is made. In a project, Claude also suggests what the series should cover next.

How do templates work?

Open a Claude Motion video you like and choose “Save as template”. Claude reads its scenes once and notes which ones stay the same, which only need new words, and which have a picture that belongs to the topic. Then type the next episodes, one per line: each gets new narration, texts, voice and captions in the same design — about 3 minutes for a 30-second video — and, if you choose, Claude redraws the topic's pictures in the same style.

Can I change a video myself?

Yes. The Design tab plays the Claude Motion video live: click a text to retype it, replace an image or a logo, fix a caption, swap a font for one with full Vietnamese accents, change a color everywhere or add a pause. Rendering the change takes a minute or two and no Claude run.

Can I finish the video in CapCut or Premiere?

Yes. Export → Layers for editing gives you each scene as its own clip, the captions on a transparent layer (and as SRT), voice, music and sound effects as separate tracks, and a timeline file for DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut. Drag them into CapCut or Premiere and keep editing.

What languages does it speak?

Scripts, voices and captions in English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Indonesian and more — with fonts that keep every diacritic intact. The app itself is in English and Vietnamese.

How long does a video take?

Quick videos take about a minute. Claude Motion designs and animates every scene and checks each frame, which usually takes 10–20 minutes — you can leave the page while it works.
