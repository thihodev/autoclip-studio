# Myth vs fact

A recipe, not rules: the user's request wins.

- **Hook:** open with the most believed myth as if it were true, then break it: "You've heard … — it's wrong."
- **Beats:** 2–4 myths. Each beat: MYTH (stated plainly, struck through or stamped ✗) → FACT (stamped ✓) → one piece of
  evidence (a number, a study, a simple demonstration). Two visual registers: a muted/warning look for myths, a clear
  confident look for facts — the same two every time.
- **Accuracy:** only facts supported by the source material or widely established; no invented statistics. If unsure,
  phrase carefully ("most studies show …").
- **Pacing:** myth fast, fact a beat slower so it sinks in.
- **End:** the one takeaway to remember, then the call to action.
